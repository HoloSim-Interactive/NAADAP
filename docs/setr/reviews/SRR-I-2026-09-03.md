## <a id="trsr-srr1-5"></a>5. Findings

1. 27 requirements approved: User Interface (UI) (1), DATA-IN (3), CORE (6), DATA-OUT (2), OUT (5), NFR (4), DELIV (9, as real line items at the Product Manager's direction). Withdrawals and additions since are recorded in the RTVM, which now carries 30 rows.
2. Public corpus: the Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Dahlgren page returned Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP) 403 from the pipeline's network; SAM.gov's public opportunities search was reachable and was recommended as the validation corpus. Adopted at Critical Design Review (CDR) (issue #6).
3. SETR artifact list: obtained from a secondary source citing Naval Air Systems Command Instruction (NAVAIRINST) 4355.19D. Superseded 2026-09-15 by the primary sources (4355.19E and the SETR Process Handbook v1.0) in the SEMP; the 19D citation in <a href="https://github.com/HoloSim-Interactive/NAADAP/blob/main/docs/RTVM.md#rtvm-deliv-960" target="_blank">DELIV-960</a> is queued for amendment at SRR-II.
