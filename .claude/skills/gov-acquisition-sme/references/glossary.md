# Acquisition glossary

Terms an acquisition professional uses without explanation. When one of
these appears in a document, it is usually a structural signal, not
decoration. Citations are to the FAR unless noted.

| DoD services portfolio groups (PGI 237.102-74) | **Confirmed against the primary source** — the 27 Aug 2012 OUSD(AT&L)/DPAP memo "Taxonomy for the Acquisition of Services and Supplies & Equipment," OCR'd from `sources/`. 9 Services Portfolio Groups (memo's own order): Research and Development; Knowledge Based Services; Logistics Management Services; Equipment Related Services; Electronic & Communication Services; Medical Services; Transportation Services; Facility Related Services; Construction Services. Total taxonomy is **16 portfolio groups / 70 portfolios**: the 9 services groups (1,497 PSC codes → 137 categories → 40 portfolios) plus 7 Supplies & Equipment groups (Aircraft/Ships-Subs/Land Vehicles; Weapons & Ammunition; Electronic & Communication Equipment; Sustainment S&E; Facilities S&E; Clothing/Textiles/Subsistence S&E; Miscellaneous S&E — 669 product codes → 203 categories → 30 portfolios). Logistics Management Services is the newest, smallest group (1 PSC, 2 categories, 2 portfolios), split out of Knowledge Based Services circa 2012. This is a 2012 PSC-mapping taxonomy for strategic-sourcing spend analysis — it predates OMB category management (2019) and DoDI 5000.74's Federal Category Structure (2020), and neither later document cross-references it. | DFARS PGI 237.102-74; 27 Aug 2012 DPAP memo |

| SEP vs. SEMP | **Two distinct, both current, different authors.** The **SEP** (Systems Engineering Plan) is the *Government program office's* plan, required by DoDI 5000.88 and structured by the OSD SEP Outline (v4.1, May 2023, in `sources/`). The **SEMP** (Systems Engineering *Management* Plan) is the *contractor's* plan, delivered under a CDRL per **DI-SESS-81785** — base 2009, Rev A 2015, **Rev B approved 08 Jan 2025, active** (ASSIST, ident 276889). The DID: the SEMP "describes the contractor's technical approach and proposed plan for the conduct, management, and control of the integrated SE effort"; format is the contractor's choice; content "shall be consistent with and address all topics in the government SEP, if available. In the absence of a government SEP, the SEMP shall address the topics in the OSD SEP Outline." The Outline itself references the SEMP as live ("align the Prime Contractor's SEMP with the PMO SEP"). **Corrected 2026-09-15:** an earlier version of this row said "SEMP is legacy terminology DoD policy has replaced with SEP; treat SEMP as superseded" — wrong. DoDI 5000.88 does not define SEMP because it governs the Government's document, not the contractor's CDRL. TEMP (Test and Evaluation Master Plan) is separately governed by DoDI 5000.89 and is not in 5000.88. | DoDI 5000.88; SEP Outline 4.1; DI-SESS-81785B |
| IPMR vs. IPMDAR | Integrated Program Management Report (older, narrative/format-based EVM report, historically DI-MGMT-81466 series) versus Integrated Program Management Data and Analysis Report (its XML/UN CEFACT-schema successor, **DI-MGMT-81861C** — confirmed against a real filled CDRL). The two are not interchangeable; a CDRL citing DI-MGMT-81861 is asking for IPMDAR, not IPMR. | verified filled CDRL in sources/ |
